PulseCo free tools

The tools run in your browser. The site does not collect or store your entries or personal data, and does not send calculator entries to a server. It has no login, analytics, tracking scripts or site cookies. Please do not enter patient identifiers.

GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may process ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. That is separate from the tools, which do all calculations on your device.

Empire HQ Pinterest app

Empire HQ only creates pins on the owner's own Pinterest account. It is not a public sign-up service and does not collect visitor or customer information through this site. The app's authorization is used only for that owner's account, not to access other people's Pinterest accounts.
